===========================================
DATABASE TABLES SUMMARY
===========================================
TABLE: students
----------------
Primary storage for student leads and their application journey from various sources (Meta, Google, Website).
A column for finding the current stage of the student in the admission process (current_student_status)
Enforces unique constraints on student_email and student_phone to prevent duplicate lead creation.
Stores contact details, educational background (highest_degree, completion_year), career status (current_profession, work_experience), and course preferences (preferred_university, preferred_course arrays).
Tracks two study modes: "Online" (distance/online learning) and "Regular" (on-campus/offline learning), default is Regular.
Manages counsellor assignments with three fields: assigned_counsellor_id (L2), assigned_counsellor_l3_id (L3), enrollment_counsellor_id.
Tracks connection status separately for L2 (is_connected_yet) and L3 (is_connected_yet_l3) counsellors.
Captures current_student_status (Fresh, Pre Application, Initial Counselling Completed, Application, Admission, Enrolled, NotInterested).
Records lead source, student age, reactivity flag (is_reactivity), and remarks_count for tracking engagement.
Central table referenced by all other tables; critical for counsellor assignment, workload distribution, and reporting on lead sources and conversion rates.
TABLE: student_remarks
-----------------------
Historical log of every call and interaction with a student, creating complete audit trail of communication attempts.
Every counsellor-student interaction generates a new remark record with unique remark_id and timestamp.
Tracks lead progression through funnel stages: Pre Application → Application → Admission → Enrolled (NotInterested also possible).
Captures calling_status (Connected/Not Connected) and sub_calling_status indicating student intent (Hot/Warm/Cold) or call outcome (Ringing no response, Line Busy, Switched off, Invalid Number).
Counsellors schedule follow-ups using callback_date field for lead nurturing and workload planning.
Records feesAmount discussed during calls and free-text remarks from counsellor observations.
To find latest status of a student: SELECT DISTINCT ON (student_id) * FROM student_remarks ORDER BY student_id, created_at DESC.
Critical for counsellor performance metrics, lead status reporting, funnel analysis, identifying bottlenecks, and callback management.
TABLE: student_lead_activities
-------------------------------
Tracks all lead generation activities and interactions with detailed marketing attribution data.
One student can have multiple lead activities, with each representing a single interaction event.
Captures complete UTM parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_keyword, utm_campaign, utm_campaign_id, utm_adgroup_id, utm_creative_id) for marketing ROI analysis.
Stores device/browser information, IP city, CTA clicked (e.g., Download PDF, Connect Now, Apply Now),  and source URL for UX optimization.
Records IVR call details including call_sid, call_recording_url, call_duration, talk_duration, ivr_status, and destination/dial numbers.
Tracks student background (highest_qualification, working_professional) and lead transfer status (is_transfered, is_transfer).
Captures parent contact (parents_number, whatsapp) and student comments in JSONB format.
Higher number of lead activities indicates higher student engagement and intent; critical for campaign optimization, conversion analysis, and marketing ROI tracking.
TABLE: counsellors
-------------------
Stores information about staff, their roles, and hierarchy in the lead management system.
Staff are divided into L2 (Juniors), L3 (Seniors), TO (Team Owners), admission_to (Admission Team Owners), and enrollment_counsellor roles.
External partners identified with PAR- prefix (is_partner = true), while internal staff use CNS- prefix.
Tracks workload metrics with total_leads (lifetime total) and current_lead_capacity (current handling capacity).
Team management enabled through assigned_to field linking counsellors to their managers/supervisors.
Status field tracks counsellor availability: active (can handle leads), inactive (cannot receive leads), or suspended.
Critical for lead distribution, counsellor performance monitoring, team structure management, and access control.
TABLE: course_status_journeys
------------------------------
Maintains complete historical audit trail of all course status changes for each student-course combination.
When counsellors discuss courses with students, all mentioned preferences are recorded (e.g., 3 colleges × 4 courses = 12 entries).
Unlike latest_course_statuses which stores only current status, this table preserves the entire journey with multiple timestamped records per student-course pair.
Tracks progression from "Shortlisted" through form submission stages (Form Filled, Form Submitted), registration, payment stages (Partially Paid, Semester Paid, Full Fee Paid), to "Enrolled" and "Active".
Records deposit amounts, currency, exam/interview dates, last admission dates, fee_type, and counsellor notes for each status change.
Links to both counsellor_id (who created the status) and enrollment_counsellor_id for tracking counsellor involvement.
Each status change creates a new record with created_at timestamp, enabling time-based analytics on admission process duration.
Essential for analyzing conversion funnel, identifying drop-off points, understanding student decision-making process, and counsellor performance analysis.
TABLE: latest_course_statuses
------------------------------
Stores the current/latest status of each course that a student has applied to or is interested in.
One record per student-course combination representing the most recent status (snapshot, not history).
Created when counsellor shortlists a course for student with default status "Shortlisted".
Links to university API for data synchronization via college_api_sent_status field (Proceed, Success, Do Not Proceed, Failed due to Technical Issues).
Includes is_shortlisted boolean flag and tracks created_by (counsellor who created the record).
Complements course_status_journeys table which maintains full historical records of all status changes.
Used by counsellors to monitor current application progress and for reporting on course popularity and enrollment rates.
TABLE: university_courses
--------------------------
Master reference table storing all university courses offered in the system with unique course_id.
Defines which universities offer which degree programs including university location (university_state, university_city), degree_name, and specialization.
Tracks academic stream (Management, Engineering, Commerce, Arts & Humanities, IT, Science), level (UG/PG/Diploma/Certificate), and study_mode (Online/Regular/Hybrid).
Stores fee structure (total_fees, semester_fees, annual_fees), course duration with duration_type (Years/Months/Semesters), and status (active/inactive).
Contains course marketing data including brochure_url, usp array (unique selling points), and eligibility array (admission criteria).
Referenced by course_status_journeys, latest_course_statuses, and student_college_credentials to track student applications and admission progress.
Foundational table for course catalog used by counsellors to shortlist courses and match student preferences with available offerings.
